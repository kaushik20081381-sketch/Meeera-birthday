My dearest Budhoo,

Another year around the sun, and somehow you are still the best part of every one of mine.

You make ordinary days feel as though they were planned just for us. A silly joke, a long call, a quiet evening, a plate of food shared between two people who both claim they are not hungry. Those are the moments I carry with me, and you are in every one of them.

I hope this year is gentle with you. I hope it brings slow mornings, big victories, small laughs, and everything you have been quietly wishing for. And I hope I am standing right beside you for as much of it as you will let me.

  • Your laughIt mends my worst day in about two seconds.
  • Your heartYou care for people more than you let on.
  • Your companyWherever you are is where I want to be.

Thank you for being you, for being patient with me, and for being mine. Now go and cut that cake, make a wish, and know that I am already working on making it come true.

Forever yours
